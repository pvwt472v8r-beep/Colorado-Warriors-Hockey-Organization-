Who we are

Our mission

Leaving the service is more than a change of clothes; it’s a profound shift in identity. The military gives you a unit, a mission, and a purpose. The civilian world often feels like a social desert where the stakes are low and nobody “gets” the dark humor or the shared shorthand of service.We use hockey to bring disabled veterans together in a supportive community. Colorado Warriors Hockey was born as a 501(c)(3) Disabled Veteran Ice Hockey Organization dedicated to those who served. We provide a space where the camaraderie of the unit is restored, and the “awkwardness” of civilian life disappears the moment the puck drops.

  • Every veteran is welcome, at every skill level
  • Peer support on and off the ice
  • Free for new players
